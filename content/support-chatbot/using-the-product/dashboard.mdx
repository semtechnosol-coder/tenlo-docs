# Dashboard at a glance.

Your dashboard lives at [app.tenloai.com/dashboard/chatbot](https://app.tenloai.com/dashboard/chatbot). The left rail has seven tabs — **Overview**, **Conversations**, **Outcomes**, **Coverage**, **Knowledge fixes**, **Tests** and **Knowledge base** — plus a **Settings** shortcut that opens the consolidated Settings hub at **Settings → Support Chatbot**.

## Overview tab

| Block | What it shows |
|---|---|
| KPI tiles | Total Conversations · Answered Rate · Human Handoffs · FAQ Entries. Answered Rate is the share whose last reply wasn't a fallback, not a resolution rate; test chats aren't counted. |
| Onboarding checklist | 8 steps; complete in order |
| Test Chat Sandbox | Try the bot with the same RAG flow real visitors hit |
| Widget install block | Your `<script>` snippet with copy button + **Verify Installation** |
| Hosted chat page link | Open the standalone chat page in a new tab |

## Conversations tab

Two-pane inbox of every chat that's happened, with filters for **All** / **Answered** / **Not answered** / **Handed off**. Mark how a handoff ended, or mark a chat as a test. Click a conversation to read the full thread and see per-message confidence scores. See [The Conversations inbox](/support-chatbot/using-the-product/conversations).

## Outcomes tab

How conversations ended, who came back with the same question, what happened to handoffs, what visitors and your team said about replies, and what your knowledge fixes changed. Counts only, no single resolution percentage. See [Outcomes](/support-chatbot/using-the-product/outcomes).

## Knowledge fixes tab

Suggested corrections to your knowledge base, from questions the bot kept missing and from replies marked wrong (a visitor's thumbs down or your **Answer was wrong**). Edit the draft, then approve or reject it. Nothing changes in your knowledge base until you approve.

## Tests tab

Save the questions you never want the chatbot to get wrong, with checks on the reply, and replay them after you change your knowledge base. **Add starter tests** adds a general set to begin with. Approving a knowledge fix reruns the tests it could affect and shows the result before and after.

## Knowledge Base tab

Four ways to add content:

- **Add FAQ** — manual question + answer entry
- **Bulk import (CSV/XLSX)** — upload typed FAQ entries in spreadsheet form
- **Add a source** — drop a URL, PDF (text-based *or scanned*), Word doc, or Markdown file and Tenlo ingests it
- **Sitemap crawl** — paste any URL from a help centre / docs site, Tenlo discovers the sitemap, and lets you tick which pages to ingest in one shot

Plus the FAQ list (with edit/delete), the imported-documents list (with status pill and delete), and the **Retrieval Inspector** card for diagnosing why the bot retrieved (or didn't retrieve) a given chunk — see [Brand voice & confidence threshold](/support-chatbot/using-the-product/brand-voice-and-confidence).

## Coverage tab

Shows where your knowledge base is falling short. Visitor questions the bot couldn't answer confidently are grouped into **gap clusters** — topics people keep asking about that your KB doesn't cover well — alongside coverage rates and a trend over the last **7, 30 or 90 days**. Work down the biggest clusters by adding FAQs or documents for them.

## Settings *(Settings → Support Chatbot)*

The dashboard's **Settings** tab is now a shortcut into the consolidated Settings hub. Its cards live at **Settings → Support Chatbot**:

| Card | Contents |
|---|---|
| General | Name, greeting, brand voice, confidence threshold, advanced retrieval |
| Handoff Rules | Handoff notification email + trigger phrases |

<Callout type="note" title="Fewer knobs than you'd expect">
The chatbot has fewer settings than you'd expect because it's grounded entirely in your KB. Most "tuning" happens by editing your FAQs, not by twisting knobs. See [Settings reference](/support-chatbot/reference/settings) for the field-by-field rundown.
</Callout>
