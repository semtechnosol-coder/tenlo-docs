# Approved follow-up emails.

Some qualified visitors leave without booking. Follow-ups give each of them one email from you, drafted by the AI and sent only after you approve it.

## What it does

- **One follow-up per lead.** Only leads scored **good fit** or **hot** that didn't book a meeting are eligible.
- **Drafted by AI, approved by you.** After the delay you choose, the AI writes a draft based on the conversation. It waits in the **Follow-ups** tab until you approve it. Nothing sends without your approval.
- **Cancelled when it no longer makes sense.** If the visitor comes back, books a meeting, or opts out before the email goes out, the follow-up is cancelled.

## Turning it on

Follow-ups are **off by default** for every business. Turn them on in the follow-up card at **Settings → Sales Assistant**, and set how long after the conversation the draft should be prepared (the delay).

## Approving drafts

Open the **Follow-ups** tab on the Sales Assistant dashboard. Each draft shows the lead and the email the AI wrote. You can edit the subject and body before you approve it. Approving sends it. Drafts you don't approve never go out.

<Callout type="note" title="One email, not a sequence">
Follow-ups are deliberately limited to a single email per lead. For multi-step outbound to people who haven't visited your site, see [Sales Outreach AI](/sales-outreach).
</Callout>
