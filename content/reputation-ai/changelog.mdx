# Changelog.

Manual changes by section, newest first.

---

## 2026-09-11

**Repackaged — Essentials, Growth and Pro, at new prices:**

| Plan | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Reputation AI Essentials (was "base") | $99 | **$30** |
| Reputation AI Growth | $199 | **$50** |
| Reputation AI Pro | $299 | **$99** |

Prices are CAD only now; the USD prices were retired. Two features moved as well:

- **Review-request campaigns** moved from the base plan to **Growth**.
- **Trust Score** and **service recovery** moved from Growth to **Pro**.

Trust assets stay in Growth; competitors and AI visibility stay in Pro, which also now carries a monthly **Trust Audit**.

**Also corrected on the billing pages:**

- **Tier changes are self-serve.** The previous note said Growth and Pro were not — they are now chosen in **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades invoice the prorated difference immediately; downgrades take effect immediately and credit the unused portion. Plan switching is deliberately absent from the Stripe portal, which still handles cards, invoices and cancellation.
- **Monthly allowances documented for the first time** — campaign emails, SMS and trust assets per tier, plus the fact that going over **holds** queued sends until the 1st rather than failing them. The 50 SMS/day cap is separate and checked first.
- **Dropping below Growth pauses queued campaign sends** rather than cancelling them, and the public intake webhook answers `403 PLAN_REQUIRED`.
- **Bundles split in two** — the Growth Bundle at $325 carries Reputation AI at Growth, the Pro Bundle at $375 carries it at Pro. The old $599 bundle maps to the Pro Bundle. Every other product's price changed too: Support Chatbot $125, Sales Outreach $125, Sales Assistant $100.
- **Multi Location** described as what it is — quoted per business, and a separate switch rather than a fourth tier.
- **Monthly report** — the ROI figures are now priced from your own numbers when you have entered them under Settings → ROI assumptions, and the report's depth follows your tier.

## 2026-08-25

**Added — the Growth and Pro features, which had shipped without documentation:**

- **Service recovery** (Growth) — where tasks come from, the board, contact logging, and the recovery script drafter. Includes the full list of things a script will never say, and why recovery is kept separate from review solicitation.
- **Trust Score** (Growth) — the six components and their weights, the grade bands, and why the number is arithmetic rather than AI. Explains coverage, which is what stops a new account reading a low early score as bad performance.
- **Trust assets** (Growth) — the five asset types, and the quote check that refuses to let an untraceable testimonial reach an approve button.
- **Competitors** (Pro) — how suggestions are ranked by closeness in review volume rather than by rating, why the first week shows a dash rather than a zero for velocity, and what the gap report does.
- **AI visibility** (Pro, Beta) — the weekly checks, how to read a result, the evidence trail, and what each outcome suggests you do. It states plainly that results are monitored samples rather than a ranking, and that no placement in an AI answer can be guaranteed.

**Corrected:**

- The billing page said Reputation AI has **"no tiers"**. It has had three since August — base ($99), Growth ($199) and Pro ($299), all flat monthly and not per-location. The plan table is now on the billing page, along with a note that Growth and Pro are not yet self-serve.

## 2026-06-14 — Settings moved to a unified hub

- **Settings consolidated** — all settings now live in one **Settings** hub (top nav). Reputation-specific settings (brand voice, routing rules, logo, platform connections, account) moved to **Settings → Reputation AI**; the Reputation page's old Settings tab is now a shortcut into the hub. Account, Notifications, and Billing are their own hub sections. Docs updated throughout — see [Settings reference](/reputation-ai/reference/settings), [Dashboard at a glance](/reputation-ai/using-the-product/dashboard), and [Quick start](/reputation-ai/getting-started/quick-start).
