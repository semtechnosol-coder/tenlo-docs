## 2026-06-14 — Settings moved to a unified hub

- **Settings consolidated** — all settings now live in one **Settings** hub (top nav). Reputation-specific settings (brand voice, routing rules, logo, platform connections, account) moved to **Settings → Reputation AI**; the Reputation page's old Settings tab is now a shortcut into the hub. Account, Notifications, and Billing are their own hub sections. Docs updated throughout — see [Settings reference](/reputation-ai/reference/settings), [Dashboard at a glance](/reputation-ai/using-the-product/dashboard), and [Quick start](/reputation-ai/getting-started/quick-start).
