# Billing & plans.

Everything billing — what you pay, how you pay it, and how to stop — is managed in **Settings → Billing** ([app.tenloai.com/dashboard/settings/billing](https://app.tenloai.com/dashboard/settings/billing)); the old `/dashboard/billing` link still works and redirects there. This page is the cross-product overview; each product's page (linked below) covers its specific limits.

## What each product costs

All prices are in CAD per month. No setup fees. Month-to-month — cancel any time.

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Reputation AI | from $30/mo — Essentials $30, Growth $50, Pro $99 |
| AI Support Chatbot | $125/mo |
| Conversational Sales Assistant | $100/mo |
| AI Sales Outreach | $125/mo — 500 prospects/mo, sends from your own Gmail/M365 mailbox |
| **Growth Bundle** | **$325/mo — all four products, Reputation AI at Growth, saves $75/mo** |
| **Pro Bundle** | **$375/mo — all four products, Reputation AI at Pro, saves $74/mo** |

Both bundles carry all four products and differ only in which Reputation AI tier comes with them:

- **Growth Bundle** — $50 + $125 + $125 + $100 = $400 bought separately, $325 together.
- **Pro Bundle** — $99 + $125 + $125 + $100 = $449 bought separately, $375 together.

Sales Outreach in either bundle includes the same 500-prospect/month cap as standalone.

**Reputation AI is the one product with tiers.** Essentials manages the reviews you already get; Growth adds the campaigns that ask for more of them, plus trust assets; Pro adds the measurement, competitive context and recovery workflow on top. See [Reputation AI — Billing & your account](/reputation-ai/reference/billing) for the full table, the monthly allowances, and what moved between tiers in September 2026.

**Reputation AI Multi Location** is quoted per business rather than priced here — email **hello@tenloai.com**.

## Free trial

New subscriptions start with a free trial. A credit card is required to begin, held securely by Stripe — Tenlo never stores your card number. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Trial length varies by product; see each product's billing page below.

## Paying, cards & invoices

Payment, cards, and invoices are all handled through the Stripe-hosted portal. From the [Billing page](https://app.tenloai.com/dashboard/settings/billing), click **Manage Billing →** to update your payment method or download past invoices as PDFs. Tenlo never sees or stores your card number, and the Stripe portal is the single source for billing history.

## Changing plans

**Reputation AI tier changes happen in the product**, not in the Stripe portal: **Settings → Billing**, pick a tier. Upgrades take effect immediately and invoice the prorated difference straight away; downgrades also take effect immediately and credit the unused part against your next invoice. The Stripe portal deliberately does not offer plan switching, so there is exactly one place a tier change happens.

To add or remove a whole product, or to move onto a bundle, email **hello@tenloai.com**.

## Cancelling

Cancel from the Billing page (**Manage Billing → Cancel plan**). Your subscription runs to the end of the current billing period — there are no refunds for remaining days. When the period ends, the product's features stop and its dashboard sections are hidden.

<Callout type="note" title="Data after cancellation">
Your data is retained while your account is open. After cancellation it is **automatically purged 90 days later** — including FAQs, conversation transcripts, leads, and your KB index. Resubscribe within that window and everything is still there. For an immediate deletion request, email hello@tenloai.com.
</Callout>
